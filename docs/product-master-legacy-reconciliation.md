# Legacy ↔ Product Master Reconciliation

**Status: READ-ONLY report. Neither side was modified.** `product_case_mappings` remains the
authority EDI reads; nothing here proposes or performs a change to it.

Generated by `scripts/reconcile_product_master_with_legacy.py` →
`analysis/master-data/product_master_legacy_reconciliation.csv`.

## Method

Both sides are joined on the **PDI item code** they already share. Wording is never part of
the join, so a description difference can never be read as a different product — it is
reported on its own axis and never escalates to a conflict.

## The 388 Master-only candidates

Answering the four questions asked of them:

- **Does the canonical product exist in the legacy system?** For **387**, yes — they are
  present in `product_identity` but carry no commercial mapping. Only **1** is absent from
  legacy master data entirely.
- **Is there no legacy commercial mapping?** Correct for all 388; that is what makes them
  master-only.
- **Is this genuinely new reference product data?** For 387, no — it is commercial data for
  products legacy already knows, sourced from distributor sheets legacy never ingested.
- **Should they be in EDI scope?** **Undetermined, and deliberately left so.** These products
  have never had an EDI mapping. Giving them one expands what the export covers rather than
  migrating what it already does, which is a business decision about scope, not a
  reconciliation outcome. None is marked EDI-eligible by this work.

## The 2 conflicts visible here

`018200967214` and `087692000570` — the master withholds a multiplier where legacy holds both
1 and 18 across stores. The other two known conflicts (`652682012217`, `865024000494`) have
no legacy counterpart, so they appear under `MASTER_ONLY` with no multiplier rather than
under `CONFLICT`.

## The 70 Legacy-only mappings

These are items EDI maps today for which the reference corpus produced no commercial
candidate — largely products invoiced by distributors whose price sheets are not in
`data/reference/`. **This is a cutover blocker**: switching authority while they are
uncovered would remove a mapping the current export depends on.
